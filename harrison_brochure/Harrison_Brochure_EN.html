Who we are

About the Clinic

Cheongdam Harrison Hospital is a specialised spine and joint hospital in Gangnam, Seoul. It was founded to bring Korea’s world-recognised expertise in spinal treatment — above all endoscopic spine surgery — to more patients, and to provide more advanced and effective care built on years of research.

Korean spine care is recognised internationally, and every year doctors from many countries come to Korea to learn endoscopic spine surgery. Harrison surgeons have developed a new endoscopic treatment for spinal stenosis, established the world’s first two-handed biportal endoscopic spine surgery technique, and train visiting foreign surgeons through the H-STEP programme.

Treatment Methods

Precise diagnosis first — then the least invasive effective treatmentEvery plan is made after a detailed examination, imaging and, when needed, a joint consultation of specialists from several centres.
A

Endoscopic spine surgery

Treats lumbar and cervical disc herniation and spinal stenosis through a minimal incision. Healthy muscles and ligaments are largely preserved; the procedure is performed under local anaesthesia while the surgeon sees the lesion directly through the endoscope with real-time imaging. Discectomy, nerve decompression and spinal fusion can all be performed endoscopically.

Advantages

  • Minimal incision — less muscle damage and scarring
  • Possible for patients with heart disease, kidney disease or diabetes
  • Suitable for elderly patients — no general-anaesthesia concerns
  • Fast recovery and early return to daily life
  • Precise: only the thickened ligament or herniated disc is removed

B

Robot-assisted joint replacement (ROSA)

The robot does not replace the surgeon: it carries out the surgeon’s plan with greater precision. Each operation is planned individually according to the joint condition, deformity and alignment — based on experience from more than 3,000 joint replacements.

  • Complication rate minimised since the robot was introduced
  • Patients in their 70s and 80s can start gait training early
  • Experience: 3,000+ knee and hip replacements

02Robot-assisted bone cutting
03Implant insertion, check of ligament balance and alignment
04Gait training and staged rehabilitation
